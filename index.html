FAQ

How is Kordon different from a captcha or from Rose and GroupHelp?

A captcha checks if someone can press a button. Kordon reads their answers and checks them against your rules. Rose and GroupHelp are good at commands, welcome messages and general moderation. Kordon handles the door: it uses Telegram’s join request guard slot, so it works next to your current bot, not instead of it. You don’t have to switch.

What admin rights does Kordon need?

Three: “Add members” (to approve join requests), “Delete messages” (to remove spam) and “Ban users” (to mute and ban spammers). The Add to group button asks for exactly these. Join requests also need to be turned on in the group settings.

What does a new member see?

When they ask to join, a short form opens right in the join request window, marked “Protected by Kordon”. They answer your 1 to 5 questions and press and hold to send. If their Telegram app doesn’t show the form there yet, Kordon sends the same form by DM. Only the chat’s admins see the answers. Nobody needs Telegram Premium.

What data does Kordon keep, and what does it never ask for?

Kordon only sees join requests and messages in chats where it is an admin. Form answers are visible to the chat’s admins and are deleted after 30 days. To review borderline cases, the text is sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini or OpenRouter). We don’t sell data.

Kordon never asks for a crypto wallet, a phone number or login codes. Fake “Safeguard”-style verification bots ask for these to steal accounts, so if a verification asks for them, it’s a scam.

What if the AI gets it wrong?

In careful mode, the default, AI never declines people on its own. Kordon only declines accounts listed in the CAS spammer database automatically. Borderline requests come to you with ✅/❌. Every decision has a reason, shown in the notification and in the decision log in the chat settings. Scam warnings come with “Delete” and “Not spam” buttons for admins, and “Not spam” keeps Kordon quiet about that person for a week.

How do I pay?

With Telegram Stars, right in the bot. Pro is 500 ⭐ and Community is 1,500 ⭐ for 30 days, and you can cancel any time. You can try Pro for 7 days for free first: once, with no payment and no auto-renewal.

Does it work with channels?

Yes. A private channel with join requests gets the same join form. To protect comments, add Kordon as an admin to the channel’s discussion group.
