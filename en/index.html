FAQ

What data does the bot see and keep?

Only join requests and messages in chats where it is an admin. Form answers are visible to the chat’s admins and are deleted after 30 days. To review borderline cases, the text is sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini or OpenRouter). We don’t sell data or collect conversations in bulk.

What if the AI is wrong?

In careful mode (the default) AI never declines people on its own: you decide borderline requests. Every decision comes with a reason.

Do I need Telegram Premium?

No — neither you nor your members.

Does it work with channels?

Yes. For a private channel with join requests — the join form. For comments — add Kordon as an admin to the channel’s discussion group.
